Bali


Bali is a popular Indonesian island and province known as the "Island of the Gods" for its rich Hindu culture, beaches, and volcanic landscapes.


Top 5 attractions in BALI :

  1. Tanah lot Temple
  2. Tegallang Rice Terrace
  3. Uluwatu Temple
  4. maunt batur
  5. Kelingking Beach

Must-try foods in Bali

  • Babi Guling (traditional roast suckling pig)
  • Nasi Campur (mixed rice with small side dishes)
  • Sate Lilit
  • Sambal Matah
